Website Planning Document

Site Name

Road 125 Adventures

This name represents motorcycle adventures made with small displacement motorcycles, showing that long journeys are possible with planning and determination.

Site Purpose

The website will share motorcycle travel experiences, route information, travel tips, equipment recommendations, and storytelling content for riders interested in long-distance travel.

Scenarios

  • What equipment is necessary for long motorcycle trips?
  • How can I plan a long trip using a small motorcycle?

Color Schema

Dark Blue (#1f3c88) – headings and navigation

Orange (#ff7a00) – accents and highlights
